Privacy Policy

New Tab – Most Visited (Chrome extension) · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This extension replaces Chrome's new tab page with a grid of your most visited sites, a weather widget, a clock, a to-do list, notes and a focus timer. We built it so that your data stays on your device. We do not run servers that receive your data, we do not sell data, and there is no advertising or analytics in the extension.

Data that stays on your device

  • Browsing history. The extension reads your Chrome history locally to count which sites you visit most and build the grid. Your history is never uploaded by the extension.
  • Settings, pinned and hidden sites are saved with Chrome storage. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome syncs these settings between your own devices through your Google account.
  • To-dos, notes, a custom background image and cached data (weather, photo list, the computed site list) are stored only in local Chrome storage on this device.
  • Backups you export are saved as a file on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Data sent to third-party services

To show icons, photos and weather, the extension contacts these services directly from your browser. They receive only what is listed, together with your IP address as with any web request.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Google favicon service, DuckDuckGo icon serviceDomain names of the sites shown in your grid (for example github.com), not full addresses or pagesTo display site icons
Open-MeteoCoordinates rounded by the service, or the city you typeWeather and forecast; city search
BigDataCloudYour coordinates (only when you use device location)To show your city name
Bing image archiveA request for the list of daily photos (no personal data)Background photos

Location is used only for weather. Chrome asks for permission first; you can deny it and type a city instead, or turn the weather widget off in Settings.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, store or sell your browsing history, to-dos or notes.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose unrelated to the features above.
  • We do not transfer data to anyone except the services listed above, and only for the purpose shown.

Your control

  • Hide any site from the grid, or limit the history period in Settings.
  • Turn off the weather widget to stop all location and weather requests.
  • Remove the extension to delete all its locally stored data.

Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
