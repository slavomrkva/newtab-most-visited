Your most visited sites, automatically — plus a clean, fully customizable dashboard. Open a new tab and see the sites you actually use, ranked from your browsing history (including history imported from other browsers). Nothing to set up, no account required.

★ FEATURES
• Most visited grid: up to 120 sites with real icons, smart ranking, drag-and-drop ordering, pin/hide, right-click to rename or add custom sites, optional "Recently visited" row and transparent icons
• Auto-hide open tabs: temporarily hides sites already open in other tabs
• Free layout editor (✥): move, resize, duplicate or hide widgets with smart snapping, alignment guides, Undo (Ctrl+Z) and Alt for free placement
• Search & Quick Jump: 10 search engines (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Perplexity, Ecosia, Startpage, Qwant, Yahoo, Yandex), instant jump to open tabs and saved sites as you type, transparent style
• World clock: 40+ timezones, 4 fonts, optional seconds, date and greeting — duplicate clocks for multiple cities
• Weather: current, hourly (24h/48h), 7-day and 14-day forecasts, multiple cities and transparent style
• To-do, Notes & Focus timer: pin To-do and Notes directly onto the page (with optional transparent background), plus a 25/50-minute Focus timer and collapsible toolbar
• Backgrounds: nature photos (daily, hourly or every tab), your own image or 6 gradients, with dimming and blur
• Sync & Backup: Chrome profile sync plus one-click JSON export/import

100% free. No ads, no account, no paywall.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your browsing history and open tabs stay on your device and are never uploaded. No ads, analytics or tracking. To-dos and notes are stored locally. Only public domain names are sent to favicon services when needed, and rounded location (~1 km) is used solely for weather (or pick cities manually / hide weather).

💡 QUICK TIPS
• Right-click any widget or site icon for its options.
• Click ✥ (bottom right) to move, resize, duplicate or hide widgets; hold Alt to overlap freely.
• Drag icons to reorder; hover to pin (📌) or hide (×).
