A clean, customizable new tab page built around the sites you actually use.

New Tab – Most Visited automatically builds a grid of your most visited websites from your browser history, including history imported from other browsers. No account or manual setup is required.

Features
• Most visited grid: 8 to 120 sites with real icons, smart recency-weighted ranking, drag-and-drop ordering, pinning, hiding, custom names, optional "Recently visited" row, and transparent icon style.
• Open-tab awareness: automatically hides sites that are already open in another tab.
• Free layout editor: move, resize, duplicate, or hide widgets with snapping, alignment guides, undo (Ctrl+Z), and Alt for unrestricted placement.
• Search and Quick Jump: 10 selectable search engines, instant switching to open tabs and saved sites as you type, and transparent style.
• World clock: 40+ time zones, 4 font styles, optional seconds, date, and greeting, with support for multiple clocks.
• Weather: current conditions, hourly (24h/48h), 7-day, and 14-day forecasts, multiple cities, and transparent style.
• Productivity tools: pinnable To-do and Notes widgets, Focus timer (25/50-minute sessions and breaks), and a collapsible toolbar.
• Backgrounds: daily, hourly, or per-tab nature photos, custom image upload, or 6 gradients, with dimming and blur controls.
• Sync and backup: Chrome profile sync plus JSON export and import.

Privacy
All browsing history and open-tab data stay on your device and are never uploaded. No ads, no analytics, and no tracking. To-do items and notes are stored locally. Only public domain names are queried for site icons when needed, and approximate location (~1 km) is used solely for weather forecasts (or cities can be set manually).

Completely free, with no ads, account, or paid tier.
